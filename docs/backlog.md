| 86 | Multipart request bodies are never inspected | Feature | v1.7.2 (Story 10.6 AC #2), deliberate. Content-type gating skips `multipart/form-data` because Lorica has no multipart parser: scanning the raw envelope means running SQL and XSS signatures over base64 and binary part payloads, which is high false-positive and low value. The follow-up is a real parser that splits the parts and applies a separate limit to the non-file ones, the ModSecurity `SecRequestBodyNoFilesLimit` model, so a form field carrying a payload is inspected while the file part next to it is not. Adjacent and same shape: a `Content-Encoding: gzip` body is not valid UTF-8 and has always passed uninspected; decompressing before the scan needs a decompression-bomb budget of its own. Both are named as known gaps in `docs/security.md`. |
